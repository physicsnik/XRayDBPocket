# Privacy Policy for XRayDB Pocket

**Effective Date**: November 20, 2026

XRayDB Pocket ("we", "our", or "us") operates the XRayDB Pocket mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
XRayDB Pocket is designed as an offline reference tool for X-ray binding energies, characteristic emission lines, and synchrotron attenuation calculations. 
- **Personal Data**: We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, user accounts, location data, or sensitive device information.
- **Offline Database**: All element tables and spectral databases are bundled locally within the app and queried entirely offline.

## 2. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)
Our App utilizes the **Google Mobile Ads SDK** to display banner advertisements. Google AdMob may collect certain device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. Children's Privacy
Our App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
