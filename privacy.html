Privacy Policy for XRayDB Pocket

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

XRayDB Pocket ("we", "our", or "us") operates the XRayDB Pocket mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information Collection and Use

XRayDBPocket is designed as an offline reference tool for X-ray binding energies, characteristic emission lines, and synchrotron attenuation calculations.

  • Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, user accounts, location data, or sensitive device information.
  • Offline Database: All element tables and spectral databases are bundled locally within the app and queried entirely offline.
